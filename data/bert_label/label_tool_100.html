Rules
  • "I" words. 1 Self: the speaker is the focus (own actions, ability, body, feelings, plans, achievements): "I made some shots", "my knee", "I'm proud of how I played".
  • 2 Team: the "I" pushes the focus or credit to the group or to others: "it's not about me", "I give credit to my teammates", "it's not just me".
  • 3 Neither: conversation or an opinion marker where the speaker is not the topic: "I think we played well", "I mean", "like I said", "I don't know".
  • In "I think I played well", the first "I" is neither and the second is self.
  • "We" words. 1 Team: the player's team (players, or players and staff). 2 Not team: any other group (family, a pair of teammates, the league, people in general).
